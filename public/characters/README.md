Pinned manifests are preserved alongside the models. `roster.json` maps game names to immutable source manifests. `sources.json` records CDN locations, sizes and SHA-256 hashes. Models are medium quality with `morphs=none`; animations are shared by body type. No credentials or guest tokens are required for these bundled assets.
